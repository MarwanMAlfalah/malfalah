Governance-aware research prototype

Vendor-Agnostic Industrial Orchestrator

Research question: How can orchestration coordinate heterogeneous industrial interfaces while preserving governance, approval boundaries, traceability and failure containment?

Method: Separate planning, capability matching, governance decisions and adapter-mediated execution, with mock OPC UA, REST and legacy interfaces, deterministic baselines and LangGraph continuation/persistence experiments.

Evidence: In the 240-scenario deterministic journal profile, both governance-aware runtimes recorded runtime-aligned outcome accuracy and policy compliance of 1.0, with zero unsafe executions under the documented definitions.

Scope: Deterministic research scenarios and mock adapters; not evidence of certified safety or live industrial deployment.

My contribution: Developed the architecture, governance/runtime implementation, evaluation design and reproducibility artifacts, with analysis and manuscript preparation.

Published research prototype

Clinical Documentation Intelligent Control System

Research question: How can clinical speech be transformed into structured documentation with supervisory control and human review?

Method: Audio → faster-whisper ASR → LLM SOAP notes → ontology-based standardization → supervisor → output, with finite-state control and a human-review path.

Evidence: Associated peer-reviewed research and a public prototype with explicit state-transition logging and supervisor decisions.

My contribution: Implemented the experimental pipeline and intelligent-control structure, integrating ASR, LLM generation, standardization and supervision.

Scope: Assistive research prototype; not an autonomous diagnostic system or deployed clinical product.

LLM evaluation project

Hallucination Detection from Hidden States

Research question: Can hidden-state probes distinguish truthful from hallucinated responses in a compact language model?

Method: SAPLMA-inspired single-best-layer probing of Qwen2.5-0.5B: extract hidden representations, select a layer by validation AUROC and train a regularized logistic-regression probe. The language model is not fine-tuned.

Evidence: On 689 labelled samples, the final probe reached internal held-out AUROC 0.664 and validation AUROC 0.727. SOLUTION.md, results.json and predictions.csv document the method, evaluation and generated predictions.

My contribution: Implemented hidden-state feature extraction and probe selection in aggregation.py and probe.py.

Scope: Internal evaluation on a small challenge dataset; no broad hallucination-detection generalization is established. This is inspired by SAPLMA, not its implementation.
