I was selected to join the SMILES 2026 International Summer School — Summer School of Machine Learning by Skoltech, jointly organized by Nanjing University and Skoltech. Current research collaboration

Intelligent monitoring methods for medical and technological processes in the operating room based on video and speech analytics

ITMO University research group · Ongoing · Targeting a Q1 journal submission

I am currently contributing to research on intelligent monitoring of medical and technological processes in the operating room using video and speech analytics. The work explores event-based multimodal analysis: combining video, speech and acoustic cues to identify temporal episodes, deviations from clinical protocols, and clinically meaningful events.

My contribution is centered on the AI/ML side of the collaboration — video/audio pipelines, event extraction, model comparison, experimental evaluation, analysis, and manuscript preparation.

Research profile

I care about what happens after the prototype works.

My research path has moved from applied machine learning and NLP toward a broader question: how can intelligent systems remain observable, reliable and useful when they operate inside complex real-world workflows?

That question now connects my work on LLM-based multi-agent systems, agent observability and evaluation, industrial orchestration, clinical documentation, hallucination detection, and multimodal medical AI.

I enjoy research where architecture, experimentation and implementation have to agree with each other — where a system is not only built, but also measured, stress-tested and explained.
